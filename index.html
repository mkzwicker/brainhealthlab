The Brain Health Lab @ StFX is a research group led by Dr. Erin Mazerolle.
Our team of collaborators and trainees advance brain science, health, and literacy through diverse perspectives and backgrounds combining education, psychology, health, biology, computer science, chemistry, engineering, physics, neuropsychology, and neurology.

Our Team

Erin Mazerolle, PhD

Principal Investigator mail emazerol@stfx.ca
download Download Full CV

Dr. Erin Mazerolle is an Associate Professor in the Department of Psychology at St. Francis Xavier University in Antigonish, Nova Scotia, where she is cross-appointed to the Department of Computer Science and Department of Biology.

Erin holds an adjunct appointment in the Department of Psychology and Neuroscience at Dalhousie University, is an Affiliate Member of Psychology at the University of Victoria, and a Scientific Affiliate (Research) of Nova Scotia Health.
